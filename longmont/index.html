Emergency Plumber Longmont, CO

Longmont, Colorado was founded in 1871 by the Chicago-Colorado Colony as the first planned community in Boulder County, and today it's the largest city in the county outside Boulder itself, with close to 100,000 residents. Historic Downtown Longmont, including landmarks like the 1881 Dickens Opera House, sits alongside decades of subsequent growth and a modern tech sector that includes employers like Seagate. The city spans both Boulder and Weld counties, extending east along US Highway 287.

Housing & Pipe History.

Longmont's Plumbing, By Era.

Historic Downtown Longmont has housing and commercial buildings dating back to the 1880s and 90s, including structures like the Dickens Opera House and the Callahan House, some with original or early-era plumbing still in place.

The much larger share of Longmont's housing was built from the mid-1900s through the present as the city grew from roughly 8,000 residents in 1950 to nearly 100,000 today, covering nearly every construction era in between.

Longmont's tech-sector growth, including long-time employers in the Longmont area, means commercial plumbing demand here is genuinely significant alongside residential service.
