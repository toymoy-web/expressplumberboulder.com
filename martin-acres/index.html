About the Neighborhood.

Where Martin Acres Sits.

Martin Acres lies east of Broadway and south of Baseline Road, running toward the US 36 corridor. Published descriptions of the exact boundary differ a little from source to source, so we describe it by its anchors rather than drawing a hard line. If you are not sure whether your street counts as Martin Acres, call and we will confirm we can reach your address.

The land was once part of William Martin's farm south of Boulder. According to a local history article, the Denver-Boulder Turnpike opened in 1952, the Martin Acres subdivision was established in 1954, and by 1960 it had grown to about 1,200 houses. That made it Boulder's largest residential subdivision up to that point.

Housing & Plumbing Context.

What the Homes Here Are Like.

Housing guides describe Martin Acres as mostly ranch houses from the 1950s and 1960s. Some owners have added second stories over the years, which is common in mid-century neighborhoods where the original floor plans were compact.

A house that is now 60 to 70 years old has usually been repaired and remodeled more than once. Age alone does not tell you what is behind the walls or under the yard, so we check what is actually installed before recommending a repair. A quote based on a guess about the neighborhood is not one we want to give you.

Additions can also change how a leak shows up. When plumbing has been extended to serve a new bathroom or a second floor, a stain on a ceiling may be several feet from the real source. That is where leak detection saves cutting open more drywall than necessary.

What We Help With.

Common Plumbing Needs for Mid-Century Homes.

Homeowners in neighborhoods like this one most often call about water heater replacement, slow or repeating drains, and fixture repairs. These are general patterns for homes of this age, not a claim about any one street.

If a drain keeps backing up, a sewer camera inspection shows the condition of the line so you can decide between cleaning and repair with real information. It is also worth doing before you buy a house here or before a major landscaping or addition project.

Basement water is a separate question. A sump pump or drain fix is plumbing work we handle. Whether a property sits in a mapped floodplain is a City of Boulder question, and the city publishes updated floodplain mapping for Bear Canyon Creek on its flood mitigation project page.

Common Questions.

Martin Acres Plumbing FAQ.

Where is Martin Acres in Boulder?

Martin Acres is in south Boulder, east of Broadway and south of Baseline Road, toward the US 36 corridor. Because published boundaries vary slightly, call us with your street address and we will confirm we can reach it.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Martin Acres?

We serve the area, which is covered by ZIP codes 80305 and 80303, from the streets around Martin Park out toward the US 36 side. Call to confirm availability for your exact address.

Are older ranch homes in Martin Acres likely to need sewer work?

Not necessarily. The age of a house does not tell us the condition of its sewer line. A camera inspection shows what is there, and that is the right first step before deciding between cleaning, spot repair, or replacement.

Does a Martin Acres address mean I am in a floodplain?

We cannot tell you that, and a plumber should not guess. The City of Boulder publishes floodplain maps and takes flood information requests for specific properties. We can handle sump pumps and drainage once you know what your property needs.

What should I do right now if a pipe is leaking in Martin Acres?

Shut off the main water valve, turn off power to any wet area at the breaker, and move belongings away from the water. Then call (303) 552-3896 and tell us what is happening so we can talk through next steps.
