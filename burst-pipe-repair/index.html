Why This Happens in Boulder.

Local Causes.

Freeze-related bursts are the most common cause in Boulder, typically in crawlspaces, exterior walls, or unheated additions, including some of the 1970s-80s construction common in Gunbarrel after a hard overnight cold snap. Bursts also happen from age-related pipe failure in older galvanized supply lines, unrelated to weather entirely.

Before you do anything else, find your main water shutoff valve and turn it fully clockwise. If water is pooling near outlets or electrical panels, shut off power to that area at the breaker rather than reaching into standing water.

Know the Signs.

Common Warning Signs.

Water actively spraying or pooling from a visible pipe section.

A sudden, dramatic drop in water pressure throughout the house.

Water stains spreading rapidly on a ceiling or wall.

The sound of rushing water inside a wall or floor.

Water pooling near the water heater or a wall with no visible source.

A section of drywall or ceiling bulging or sagging from trapped water.

How We Diagnose It.

Finding the Actual Problem.

Once water is shut off to the affected section, we locate the exact break and assess whether it's an isolated failure or a sign the surrounding pipe is also compromised. Repairing one visible break while ignoring an aging line nearby just delays the next call.

We also check whether water reached areas beyond the immediate break, since a pipe that sprayed for even a few minutes before being shut off can soak more of the surrounding structure than is obvious at first glance. Repairs themselves follow the same International Plumbing Code workmanship standards Colorado has adopted statewide, not a shortcut fix.

Common Questions.

Straight Answers.

What's the first thing I should do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main water valve immediately. That single step stops the situation from getting worse while you wait for a technician, and we can talk you through finding the valve over the phone if you're not sure where it is.

Will a burst pipe cause water damage even after the leak is fixed?

It can, especially if water ran for a while before it was noticed. Once the pipe itself is repaired, it's worth having any soaked drywall, flooring, or insulation assessed separately for drying and water damage concerns.

How quickly can you get to a burst pipe emergency?

This is treated as a genuine emergency call, not a scheduled appointment. Call the number above and we'll get a technician moving toward you right away, walking you through immediate shutoff steps in the meantime.

Can a burst pipe mean other pipes nearby are about to fail too?

Sometimes, especially if the burst was caused by age rather than an isolated freeze event. That's exactly why we look at the surrounding pipe material and condition rather than only patching the visible break, and we'll flag anything else worth watching.
