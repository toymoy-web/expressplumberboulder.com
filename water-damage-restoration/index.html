Water Damage Restoration in Boulder, CO

Water damage restoration is a different service from plumbing repair. It's about extracting standing water, drying out affected structure and contents, and preventing mold before it starts, regardless of what caused the water in the first place. Boulder Express Plumbing coordinates water damage restoration across Boulder, working alongside plumbing repair when a plumbing failure is the cause.

Why This Happens in Boulder.

Why It Happens Here.

Mold risk in Boulder's climate genuinely accelerates once materials stay wet, typically becoming a real concern within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure. That timeline is exactly why extraction and drying need to start quickly rather than waiting for a convenient time.

Before help arrives, avoid running electrical equipment in or near standing water, and if it's safe to do so, move furniture and belongings away from the wet area. Take photos of the damage before anything is moved, since that documentation matters for any insurance claim. Our extraction and drying process follows the water damage restoration standards published by the IICRC, the recognized industry body for this work.

Know the Signs.

Signs to Watch For.

Standing water from a burst pipe, appliance failure, or roof or storm intrusion.

Visible water stains spreading on walls, ceilings, or flooring.

A musty smell developing in an area that got wet.

Warped flooring or bubbling paint after water exposure.

Wet drywall, insulation, or subfloor that hasn't been addressed.

Water damage discovered after returning from time away from the property.

How We Diagnose It.

How We Track Down the Cause.

We use moisture meters to find water that's migrated beyond what's visible, since water damage often extends further into walls and subfloor than the visible wet area suggests. That mapping determines exactly what needs to be dried versus what can be left alone.

We document the affected areas and cause thoroughly, which is genuinely useful if you're filing an insurance claim, separate from the drying work itself.

Materials that can be dried in place are dried rather than automatically removed, since unnecessary demolition adds cost without improving the outcome when the material itself is salvageable.

Common Questions.

Questions We Get Often.

How fast does mold actually start growing after water damage?

Conditions favorable to mold growth can develop within 24 to 48 hours of materials staying wet, which is why we prioritize starting extraction and drying immediately rather than scheduling it like a routine repair.

Will insurance cover water damage restoration?

Many policies cover sudden, accidental water damage, though coverage details vary and we can't speak to your specific policy. We document the cause and extent thoroughly to support whatever claim you file.

Is water damage restoration the same as the plumbing repair that caused it?

No, they're separate services. If a burst pipe caused the damage, we handle the pipe repair separately from the extraction and drying work, and we coordinate both so nothing falls through the gap.

Do I need to remove wet drywall myself before you arrive?

No, please don't. Removing materials yourself can complicate insurance documentation and isn't necessary for safety in most cases. Leave it for us to assess and document properly first.

How long does drying out a water-damaged area take?

It depends on how much material got wet and how long it sat before drying started, but a few days of equipment running is common for a moderate-sized area.
