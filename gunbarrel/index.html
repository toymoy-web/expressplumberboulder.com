Local Neighborhoods.

The Pockets of Gunbarrel We Work In.

Gunbarrel covers a few distinct pockets, including Gunbarrel, Country Club Estates, and Twin Lakes, each with its own construction era and quirks.

Homes built during Gunbarrel's original 1970s-80s construction wave are reaching the point where original water heaters, past any reasonable service life, are the most common reason for a service call, more than any leak or emergency issue.

Properties near Twin Lakes Open Space, a former gravel pit restored into wetland, sit closer to a higher water table than homes further from the lakes, which is worth knowing for anything involving a sump pump or crawlspace.

Housing & Pipe History.

Gunbarrel's Plumbing, By Era.

Gunbarrel was largely farmland until IBM built a facility here in 1965, which drove the neighborhood's main residential construction boom through the 1970s and 80s. Country Club Estates, a 169-home subdivision, was established later in 1996, adding a slightly newer construction pocket into the same general area.

Most Gunbarrel homes still run on PEX and copper supply lines typical of 1970s-90s construction, generally newer than the rest of Boulder's housing stock but old enough now for water heaters and fixtures from the original construction wave to be reaching end of service life.

Gunbarrel sits along the Diagonal Highway corridor at a slightly lower, flatter elevation than central Boulder, and Twin Lakes Open Space nearby was originally a set of gravel pits later restored into wetland, which affects groundwater behavior for properties near it.

Common Questions.

Gunbarrel Plumbing FAQ.

Is Gunbarrel's plumbing newer than the rest of Boulder?

Generally, yes. Most of the neighborhood dates to the 1970s and 80s rather than Boulder's much older historic core, so PEX and copper are far more common here than the galvanized pipe found in pre-1940s neighborhoods.

Does being near Twin Lakes affect my plumbing?

Properties closest to the open space can see a higher water table, which is more relevant to sump pump and crawlspace moisture questions than to everyday fixture repairs.

Do you service commercial properties in Gunbarrel?

Yes, Gunbarrel has a real mix of residential and commercial properties along the Diagonal Highway corridor, and we handle both under the same commercial plumbing service.

Is Gunbarrel technically part of Boulder?

It's an unincorporated area of Boulder County rather than the city proper. Much of it is on city of Boulder water and sewer service, and we treat it as a core part of our regular service area either way, with the same response time and pricing as anywhere else we cover.
