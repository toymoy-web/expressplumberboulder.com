Emergency Plumber Lafayette, CO

Lafayette, Colorado was platted in 1888 by Mary Miller, who named the town after her late husband, and incorporated the following year. Like its neighbor Louisville, Lafayette grew up as a coal-mining town before transitioning to the suburban community it is today, which means Old Town Lafayette's older housing sits alongside decades of subsequent suburban development out toward Waneka Lake and beyond. Today the city is home to tens of thousands of residents across a genuinely wide range of housing ages.

Housing & Pipe History.

Lafayette's Plumbing, By Era.

Old Town Lafayette, near the historic core platted in 1888, has the oldest housing stock in the city, some of it dating to Lafayette's coal-mining boom in the early 1900s.

The much larger share of Lafayette's housing was built from the mid-1900s onward as the city grew into a Boulder County suburb, which means copper and PEX plumbing is the norm outside the historic core.

Newer development around Waneka Lake and the city's eastern edge toward US 287 represents some of Lafayette's most recent construction, with plumbing systems that are still well within their expected service life.

Local Neighborhoods.

Lafayette's Neighborhoods We Work In.

Lafayette covers a real mix of eras, from Old Town's original coal-era homes to newer subdivisions built out toward Waneka Lake and the city's eastern edge.
