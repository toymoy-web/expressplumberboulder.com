Why This Happens in Boulder.

The Boulder Factor.

A lot of general repair calls in Boulder, especially in mixed-era pockets like University Hill, trace back to the age and mix of plumbing in a given house. It's common for a home to have copper, galvanized, and PEX all present in different sections after decades of piecemeal updates, and knowing which material you're dealing with in a given spot changes the right fix.

Homes that have gone through multiple renovations over the decades sometimes have plumbing modifications that were never fully documented, which is part of why a walkthrough matters more than working from assumptions about what should be behind a wall.

Know the Signs.

What to Look For.

An unexplained increase in your water bill.

A shutoff valve that won't fully close or is stuck.

Low water pressure at one fixture but not others.

Unusual noise in the pipes (banging, whistling, or knocking).

A fixture or appliance connection that seems off but isn't a clear emergency.

A plumbing quirk you've lived with for a while but never had checked.

How We Diagnose It.

Getting to the Root Cause.

For a vague or unusual complaint, we start with a straightforward walkthrough and pressure check rather than assuming which system is at fault. A lot of general repair calls turn out to be simple once we actually look, even when the symptom seemed confusing over the phone.

If the issue turns out to be something outside general repair, like a hidden leak or sewer line issue, we'll tell you and point you to the right specific service rather than guessing at a fix that won't hold.

Banging or knocking pipe noise, sometimes called water hammer, is a common example of a symptom that sounds alarming but is often a quick fix once we identify which fixture is causing the pressure spike. All repairs follow the same International Plumbing Code workmanship standards Colorado has adopted statewide, regardless of how small the fix is.

Common Questions.

What Homeowners Ask.

What if I'm not sure what's actually wrong?

That's exactly what a general plumbing call is for. Describe what you're noticing and we'll take it from there rather than needing you to diagnose it in advance.

Do you handle small repairs, or only bigger jobs?

We handle both. A small fix done properly now is usually cheaper than the same issue left to grow into something bigger later.

What's water hammer, and is it serious?

It's the banging or knocking sound pipes make when a valve closes quickly and sends a pressure shockwave through the line. It's not usually an emergency, but over years it can stress fittings, so it's worth having checked rather than just living with the noise.

Do you charge for a diagnostic visit if it turns out to be nothing serious?

We charge for the visit itself, but we'd rather confirm something is fine than have you live with uncertainty. We'll always tell you honestly if nothing needs fixing.
