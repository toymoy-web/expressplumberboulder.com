Hydro Jetting in Boulder, CO

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clear a line completely rather than punching a hole through a clog the way cabling does. Boulder Express Plumbing uses it for lines with grease buildup, root intrusion, or a clog that keeps coming back after regular snaking.

Why This Happens in Boulder.

Why Boulder Homes See This.

Older clay sewer laterals in North Boulder's established neighborhoods are especially prone to this pattern, since root intrusion coats the inside of the pipe over time in a way that cabling only punches through rather than actually clearing.

Restaurants and older kitchens with years of grease going down the drain see a similar pattern on a shorter timeline. Grease builds up in layers along the pipe wall, and each round of cabling just punches a new hole through the middle of it.

Know the Signs.

Warning Signs to Know.

A clog that returns within weeks of being cabled.

Cabling getting stuck or unable to reach the full length of the line.

Grease buildup in a kitchen line, especially in older homes.

Multiple slow drains suggesting a main line issue.

A camera inspection that shows significant buildup along the pipe wall.

A line that's never been cleaned since the home or building was built.

What's Actually Going On.

It Got Stuck at 60 Feet and the Clog Keeps Coming Back.

A common way this call comes in: a previous attempt at cabling either got stuck partway down the line or cleared the immediate clog only for it to return within weeks. Both patterns usually point to something cabling alone can't fully address, root intrusion coating the pipe wall or a section with a genuine structural issue rather than a simple blockage.

How We Diagnose It.

How We Diagnose the Issue.

We camera-inspect first whenever possible to confirm hydro jetting is actually the right tool for the pipe's condition and material. Jetting an already-compromised pipe at the wrong pressure can cause damage, so matching the equipment to what we actually see in the line matters.

Pressure settings are adjusted for the specific pipe material and its condition, since an older, more fragile line needs a gentler approach than a newer PVC line with heavy grease buildup.

Common Questions.

Common Questions, Answered.

Is hydro jetting better than snaking?

For a simple, isolated clog, snaking is often enough and less expensive. For grease buildup or root intrusion that keeps coming back, jetting clears the pipe wall itself rather than just punching a path through, which is why it holds up longer.

Can hydro jetting damage old pipes?

It can, if the pressure isn't matched to the pipe's actual condition and material. That's exactly why we camera-inspect first on older lines rather than jetting blind.

How often should a line be hydro jetted for maintenance?

For most homes, this isn't a routine annual service, it's used when there's an actual buildup problem. Restaurants and commercial kitchens are the exception, where scheduled jetting is often worth doing to prevent grease-related backups before they start.

How long does hydro jetting take?

Most residential lines take under two hours including setup, though a heavily built-up or longer commercial line can take longer.
