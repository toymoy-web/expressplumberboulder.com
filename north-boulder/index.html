Housing & Pipe History.

North Boulder's Plumbing, By Era.

Mapleton Hill was designated Boulder's first historic district in 1982, and roughly 57% of its homes were built before 1910 in Victorian, Queen Anne, and Craftsman styles. Newlands and Old North Boulder fill in with a mix of early 1900s bungalows and later infill.

Most North Boulder homes still run on original galvanized steel supply lines and, in some homes, clay sewer laterals that have never been replaced since construction.

Mapleton Hill was built on the hill specifically because of its good natural drainage, which is a real historical reason the area was settled first, not a plumbing detail we're inventing after the fact.

Common Issues.

What We're Called About Most in North Boulder.

The single most common call we get from North Boulder is Water Heater Repair, because aging galvanized supply lines and original masonry chimney venting drive a disproportionate share of water heater calls here compared to newer parts of the city.

Recurring sewer line clogs from clay laterals with root intrusion at aging joints.

Low water pressure throughout an older home on original supply lines.

Local Neighborhoods.

The Pockets of North Boulder We Work In.

North Boulder covers a few distinct pockets, including Mapleton Hill, Newlands, and Old North Boulder, each with its own construction era and quirks.

Because Mapleton Hill is a designated historic district, exterior changes to these homes require a Landmark Alteration Certificate from the city, which doesn't apply to interior plumbing work but is worth knowing if a repair also touches anything visible from the street. Call ahead and we can tell you whether your specific job needs that extra step.

Newlands and Old North Boulder, just north and west of Mapleton Hill proper, have a similar mix of early-1900s construction with more mid-century infill mixed in, so pipe material varies more block to block than in Mapleton Hill's tighter historic core. We check the actual pipe material on-site before quoting a repair rather than assuming based on the block.

Common Questions.

North Boulder Plumbing FAQ.

Do North Boulder's historic district rules affect plumbing repairs?

Interior plumbing work generally doesn't require the Landmark Alteration Certificate that exterior changes need in Mapleton Hill. If a repair requires cutting into an exterior wall or visible feature, that's worth flagging with the city first, and we can point you in the right direction.

How do I know if my North Boulder home still has galvanized pipe?

Homes built before roughly 1960 in this area commonly still have some original galvanized supply line, especially if it hasn't been part of a past renovation. We can confirm what you have during a general plumbing repair or leak detection visit.

Does Mapleton Hill's historic status make plumbing repairs more expensive?

Not for interior work. The Landmark Alteration Certificate requirement applies to exterior changes, so a standard repair or replacement inside the home doesn't typically trigger any additional city review.

Do you work in Newlands and Old North Boulder too, not just Mapleton Hill?

Yes, all of North Boulder, including Newlands and Old North Boulder, is part of our regular service area, and each of those pockets gets the same attention to its specific construction era.
