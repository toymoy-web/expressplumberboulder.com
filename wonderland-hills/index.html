About the Neighborhood.

Where Wonderland Hills Sits.

Wonderland Hills is in north Boulder, in ZIP code 80304, roughly three miles north of Downtown by one housing guide's estimate. It is organized around Wonderland Lake Park on Poplar Avenue west of Broadway. The City of Boulder describes that park as having a playground, shelter, open turf, a multi-use path, and access to the lake and nearby Open Space and Mountain Parks trails.

Exact boundaries for the neighborhood are not formally published, so we use Wonderland Lake and Poplar Avenue as anchors. Call with your address if you are unsure whether it falls inside the area we usually describe this way.

Housing & Plumbing Context.

What the Homes Here Are Like.

Published housing data describes a mix of single-family homes and townhomes. One neighborhood data source says many homes were built between 1970 and 1999 with some newer, and another reports a median build year of about 1983 with most homes from the 1940s through the 1990s. The two agree that this is mostly late-20th-century housing.

Homes in that range are old enough that original water heaters, toilets, faucets, and disposals have often been replaced once, and some are due again. That is a pattern for the age group, not a claim about a specific house.

Because these homes are newer than the downtown core, the first question is usually what has already been updated and what has not. We start there instead of assuming a full repipe or sewer replacement is needed.

What We Help With.

Common Plumbing Needs for Late-20th-Century Homes.

Expect most calls to involve water heater replacement, toilet repair, faucet and sink repair, or a garbage disposal that has reached the end of its life. Fixing a worn part is often the better call than replacing the whole fixture.

Being close to the foothills means winter cold arrives early. Any line in an unheated garage, crawlspace, or exterior wall deserves attention before a hard freeze, and frozen pipe repair is available if one does let go.

The City of Boulder reported in October 2024 that its inventory of drinking water service lines found no lead service lines. That announcement covers the city's service lines. It does not describe the plumbing inside any individual home, so we still look at what is actually installed.

Common Questions.

Wonderland Hills Plumbing FAQ.

Where is Wonderland Hills in Boulder?

Wonderland Hills is in north Boulder, around Wonderland Lake Park on Poplar Avenue west of Broadway, in ZIP code 80304. Boundaries are informal, so call with your address and we will confirm we can reach it.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Wonderland Hills?

The neighborhood is covered by ZIP code 80304, and we serve it from the streets near Wonderland Lake out through the surrounding north Boulder blocks. Call to confirm availability for your exact address.

My Wonderland Hills home was built in the 1970s or 1980s. Does it need a repipe?

Not automatically. A home of that age may have all original plumbing, a mix of old and new, or a fully updated system. We confirm what is installed and what condition it is in before suggesting anything larger than a repair.

Should I replace a water heater that still works?

Water heaters do wear out, and one that has served for many years is worth checking. We look at its age, condition, and venting, then explain whether repair or replacement makes more sense for your household.

Is a lead service line a concern in Boulder?

The City of Boulder said in October 2024 that its service line inventory found no lead service lines. That covers city service lines, not the pipes inside a home. For questions about your specific property, the city's water service line inventory page is the best place to check.
