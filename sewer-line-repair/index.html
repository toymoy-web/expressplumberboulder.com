Why This Happens in Boulder.

Why Boulder Homes See This.

Older clay sewer laterals in Boulder's established neighborhoods are especially prone to root intrusion at pipe joints, where tree roots find moisture and gradually work their way in over years. A single root intrusion point can be spot-repaired. A line with intrusion at multiple points usually points toward a fuller repair or lining.

Boulder's expansive clay soil also contributes independently of tree roots, since ground movement can offset pipe joints or crack sections of pipe over time even without any nearby vegetation.

Know the Signs.

Warning Signs to Know.

Sewage backing up into a floor drain or basement fixture.

Multiple drains backing up at the same time.

A gurgling sound from drains when the toilet is flushed.

Sewage smell in the yard, especially near a known line path.

A previous camera inspection that found a specific crack or root intrusion point.

A soggy or unusually green patch of lawn above the suspected line path.

What's Actually Going On.

There's a Crack With Root Intrusion, and I Need a Spot Repair.

This is a common way a sewer line call comes in once a homeowner has already had some diagnosis done, whether from a previous cleaning or their own research. A localized crack with root growth inside it needs a repair, not another round of cabling. A spot repair addresses the specific damaged section without replacing the whole line, which is often the right call when the rest of the pipe is in reasonable shape.

How We Diagnose It.

How We Diagnose the Issue.

We camera-inspect before recommending a repair method, since the right fix depends entirely on where the damage is and how extensive it is. A single localized crack calls for a different approach than a line failing at multiple points along its length.

If the line is too clogged for a clear camera view, we'll clear it first, usually with hydro jetting, so the inspection actually shows us the pipe's real condition rather than just the blockage sitting in front of the lens. Any repair method we use meets the same International Plumbing Code standards Colorado has adopted statewide.

Common Questions.

Common Questions, Answered.

What's the difference between a spot repair and relining a whole sewer line?

A spot repair fixes one specific damaged section. Relining or replacement addresses the entire line's condition. We recommend one over the other based on what the camera inspection actually shows, not a default assumption.

Can tree roots really crack a sewer pipe?

Roots more often find their way into an existing crack or loose joint than crack a sound pipe outright. Once inside, they grow and expand the damage over time, which is why a small intrusion point tends to get worse rather than better.
