Emergency Plumber Superior, CO

Superior, Colorado was founded in 1896 and incorporated in 1904 as a coal-mining town. The December 2021 Marshall Fire, the most destructive wildfire in Colorado history, swept through Original Town Superior and destroyed the vast majority of its historic homes, with only a handful surviving. The years since have been defined by an extensive rebuilding effort, which means Superior's housing stock, more than almost any other city in the area, is now genuinely new. Thousands of residents were evacuated the night of the fire, and the rebuild has continued for years since.

Housing & Pipe History.

Superior's Plumbing, By Era.

Original Town Superior, the small historic core near the old coal-mining site, lost nearly all of its pre-fire homes in the Marshall Fire, and most of what stands there today has been rebuilt since 2022.

The rest of Superior's housing, including established subdivisions that weren't in the fire's direct path, is mostly 1990s-and-later suburban construction, newer than much of the surrounding area.

Because so much of Superior's current housing is brand new, the plumbing issues we see here skew toward new-construction questions and code compliance rather than the aging-infrastructure problems common in older cities.
