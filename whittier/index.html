About the Neighborhood.

Where Whittier Sits.

Whittier is a few blocks northeast of Downtown Boulder, around Whittier Elementary at 2008 Pine Street. One local neighborhood guide places it roughly east of Broadway, north of Canyon Boulevard, and west of 28th Street. Treat that as approximate, since boundaries are not formally fixed and other sources draw them a little differently.

The neighborhood grew in the late 1800s as Boulder spread east from its original town site. The school opened in the early 1880s as the Pine Street School and was renamed Whittier in 1903, after students corresponded with the poet John Greenleaf Whittier. Sources vary on whether it opened in 1882 or 1883, so we stay with the early 1880s.

Housing & Plumbing Context.

What the Homes Here Are Like.

A local neighborhood guide describes Whittier housing as Victorian cottages, Queen Anne homes, Arts and Crafts bungalows, and brick bungalows from the 1910s and 1920s. It also notes that many larger houses were converted to duplexes or triplexes, with condos and townhomes toward Canyon Boulevard and 28th Street.

That mix matters for plumbing. In a converted house, drains and water lines often serve more than one unit, so finding which unit a leak or clog comes from takes tracing, not guessing. In older cottages, additions and remodels from different decades can mean different pipe materials in different parts of the same house.

We do not assume what is in a Whittier home based on its age or style. We confirm it on site, then explain what we found before you decide on anything.

What We Help With.

Common Plumbing Needs for Older and Converted Homes.

A recurring clog in an older house is a good case for a sewer camera inspection, which shows the condition of the line before you pay for cleaning that may not hold. For slow or repeated blockages, drain cleaning is the usual starting point.

When a ceiling stain or a damp wall has no obvious source, leak detection narrows the search before anyone opens a wall. In small cottages and basements with tight access, water heater repair and replacement call for careful planning about space and venting.

If your home is a designated landmark or sits in a historic district, exterior changes may need city review. Interior plumbing is a separate matter, and we can tell you when a job would touch something visible from the street so you can check with the city first.

Common Questions.

Whittier Plumbing FAQ.

Where is Whittier in Boulder?

Whittier is just northeast of Downtown Boulder, around Whittier Elementary on Pine Street. One local guide places it roughly between Broadway and 28th Street, north of Canyon Boulevard. Boundaries are informal, so call us with your address and we will confirm we can reach it.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Whittier?

We serve the neighborhood, which is covered by ZIP codes 80302 and 80304, from the blocks near Broadway out toward 28th Street. Call to confirm availability for your exact address.

My Whittier house was split into apartments. Does that change plumbing repairs?

It can. Shared drain lines and water lines mean a problem in one unit may come from another. We trace the source first, then talk through who needs to be involved, such as a landlord or another owner, before any repair begins.

Should I get a sewer camera inspection before buying an older Whittier home?

It is a reasonable step for any older house. The camera shows the actual condition of the sewer line, which beats guessing from the house's age. We can walk through what the footage shows before you make an offer decision.

Does historic status affect my plumbing repair?

Exterior changes to a landmarked home or a home in a historic district may need city review. Inside the house is a different matter. Tell us if a repair would affect anything visible from outside, and we will flag it so you can check with the city.
