Why This Happens in Boulder.

The Boulder Factor.

Older Boulder homes, particularly in the Mapleton Hill and University Hill areas, sometimes still run on original clay sewer laterals. Tree roots find their way into the joints of these older lines over decades, and what looks like a simple clog is often root intrusion that keeps coming back until the root growth itself is cleared.

Kitchen drains are the other recurring culprit, especially in homes with garbage disposals handling more grease and food waste than the line was designed for. Grease cools and hardens against the inside of the pipe long before it reaches the street, narrowing the line gradually until a clog finally forms.

Know the Signs.

What to Look For.

Water draining slower than normal in one or more fixtures.

Gurgling sounds from a drain when another fixture is used.

Multiple drains backing up at the same time (a main-line symptom).

A recurring clog that comes back a few weeks after snaking.

Sewage smell near a floor drain or basement fixture.

How We Diagnose It.

Getting to the Root Cause.

For a single slow drain, cabling usually clears it in one visit. When a clog keeps recurring at the same spot, or multiple fixtures back up together, we run a video sewer camera down the line first to see whether it's a simple blockage or root intrusion in an aging clay lateral.

The camera also tells us the pipe's actual material and condition, which matters for older Boulder homes where a previous owner may have done a partial repair with a different pipe type. Mixed materials in one line is common enough in older housing stock that we check rather than assume.

Your Options.

Fixing It the Right Way.

Drain Snaking / Cabling.

  • Standard fix for a single slow or clogged drain.
  • Fast, usually same-visit.
  • Doesn't remove root growth from the pipe walls.

Hydro Jetting.

  • High-pressure water clears grease buildup and root intrusion the cable can't reach.
  • Better fit for a clog that keeps coming back.
  • Recommended after a camera inspection confirms it's the right tool for the pipe's condition.

Common Questions.

What Homeowners Ask.

Why does my drain keep clogging in the same spot?

A drain that clogs at the same spot repeatedly, weeks or months apart, usually means something structural in the pipe itself, most often root intrusion in an older clay lateral. Cabling clears the immediate blockage but not the roots growing into the joint. That's when hydro jetting or a camera inspection makes sense.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

For most pipe materials, yes, when the pressure is matched to the pipe's actual condition, which is exactly why we camera-inspect first on older lines rather than jetting blind.

Can I use store-bought drain cleaner before calling you?

We'd rather you didn't. Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipe materials and make it harder and more hazardous for a technician to cable or camera the line afterward. A plunger is fine for a single fixture. Beyond that, call us.
